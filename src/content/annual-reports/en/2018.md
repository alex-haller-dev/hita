## Main activities

- Technical design of the project “Campus-WLAN + Computer Lab for School of Nursing and Midwifery, UHAS, Ho” by HITA experts; Creation of procurement lists for required infrastructure components and comparison with list of donated goods (January-February 2018)
- Planning of the logistical handling of the transport of the donated goods by container shipping (January-March 2018)
- Participation in the information event on the BMBF funding initiative “Research on the Internationalization of Vocational Training”, Bonn (January 30, 2018)
- Preparatory discussions and measures for a cooperation between HITA and the Carlo Mierendorff School, Frankfurt. Content-related support for activities through which teachers working there inform their school classes about the situation in Ghana and design and implement concrete aid measures (e.g. donation of toys and sports equipment) (April-December 2018)
- Implementation of the campus WLAN and the computer lab at the School of Nursing and Midwifery (SONAM/UHAS) in Ho: Viewing and analysis of the infrastructure already installed at SONAM (IT components including networking, premises, furniture) and corresponding adjustment of the technical concept; Installation of all required components in cooperation with the IT specialists at UHAS; testing and trial operation; training measures to ensure the systems are properly maintained; Official opening of the HITA computer lab and the WLAN together with the rector of the university; Arrangements to ensure effective cooperation between the SONAM team and HITA in the coming months (May 17-31, 2018)
- Conception, implementation and evaluation of individual and group interviews for data collection for the evaluation of the effects of the intervention “Implementation of a campus WLAN and computer labs at SONAM” (May 17-31, 2018)
- Distribution of donated medical supplies to selected health facilities in Ho and Hasu, Volta Region (May 17-31, 2018)
- Distribution of the toys and sports equipment donated by the Carlo Mierendorff School to selected schools in Ho and Hasu, Adaklu District (May 17-31, 2018)
- Enthronement of HITA CEO Thomas Erkert as “Development Chief” of the Adaklu District at a festive ceremony in Hasu, as an expression of recognition of HITA’s achievements already made and those planned for the future (May 25, 2018)
- Participation in the conception and preparation of a project application (“1Step4Life – Co-creative mHealth solution to improve maternal and child health care in Sub-Saharan African rural communities”) as part of the EU research program Horizon 2020 (Call: H2020-SC1-BHC- 2018-2020) in cooperation with a consortium led by Turku University of Applied Sciences (TUAS). Participation in a 1.5-day preparatory meeting in Turku (April-October 2018)
- Opening of an account on the WeCanHelp platform and on Amazon Smile to acquire donations using innovative crowdfunding methods (September 2018)
